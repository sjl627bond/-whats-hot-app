PRIVACY POLICY

Your night.
Your choices.

Effective August 19, 2026 · Pre-launch policy

At a glance

Location is optional. Precise location evidence is not displayed publicly. GoHott does not sell personal information or use third-party advertising SDKs in the current application.

1. Scope and contact

This policy describes the current GoHott nightlife discovery web and iOS application. GoHott is still in launch preparation; the final operating entity and monitored customer-support address must be published before public App Store release. Until then, use the Support page for the available pre-launch channel.

2. Information you provide

  • Account and authentication: email address, password-based authentication records managed by Supabase Auth, session information, and account identifiers.
  • Profile: optional display name, username, avatar URL, bio, home city, favorite categories, profile visibility, messaging preference, and notification preferences.
  • Nightlife activity: saved venues, crowd reports/check-ins, crowd level and vibe, nightlife plans and their chosen visibility, follows, blocks, reactions, reports, and notification state.
  • Messages: direct-message text and optional references to venues, profiles, Live Looks, or plans when messaging is enabled.
  • Live Look: a still photo selected from your camera or photo library, optional caption, selected expiration duration, file type, file size, and a content fingerprint used for abuse prevention. The current app does not accept video uploads.
  • Requests: account deletion and data-export requests, plus moderation reports and the reason you select.

3. Location

GoHott asks for foreground location only after showing an explanation and receiving your choice. If allowed, the app may use coordinates and accuracy to calculate distance to venues and submit proximity evidence for authenticated check-ins or Live Looks. Server-side logic may assess whether an action occurred near a venue.

Precise evidence is kept in restricted database records, is not included in public feeds, and is designed for short retention. Venue discovery, city switching, and most browsing continue when location is denied, restricted, or unavailable. You can revoke permission in iOS Settings or your browser’s site settings.

4. Venue discovery and rankings

GoHott processes venue records, verified coordinates and metadata, recent crowd reports, Live Looks, and nightlife-plan signals to display discovery results, distances, maps, and explainable “hot” rankings. These results describe recent activity and may be incomplete or delayed.

5. Device and technical information

The application necessarily exchanges network requests, IP addresses, timestamps, and basic HTTP/device information with hosting and backend providers. GoHott’s current client error monitor keeps sanitized error text, route, and time in a temporary in-memory queue on your device; the current browser code does not transmit that queue to GoHott. The app does not currently include an advertising tracker.

6. How information is used

  • Operate authentication, profiles, discovery, maps, saves, check-ins, Live Looks, social features, messages, notifications, and sharing.
  • Calculate distance, proximity assessments, rankings, and activity freshness.
  • Enforce privacy settings, blocks, rate limits, duplicate protection, content expiration, and access controls.
  • Review reports, investigate abuse, secure the service, troubleshoot failures, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Process export and deletion requests once the required protected backend workers are enabled.

7. When information is shared

Information is shared with other users only according to the feature and your choices—for example, public crowd reports, active Live Looks, visible profiles, selected plan visibility, or messages visible to conversation participants. Precise location evidence is not shared publicly.

Service providers process information to operate GoHott: Supabase provides authentication, PostgreSQL database, Realtime, and private media Storage; Vercel hosts and delivers the web application; and OpenStreetMap tile infrastructure receives map-tile requests when the map loads. Apple and your device operating system manage native permissions and, when enabled later, push-notification delivery. Providers process data under their own terms and security practices.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary for legal compliance, safety, fraud prevention, or protecting rights. GoHott does not currently sell personal information.

8. Security

GoHott uses HTTPS, Supabase authentication, row-level security, restricted database grants, private media storage with short-lived signed URLs, validated identifiers, and separation of privileged server credentials from browser code. No system is perfectly secure; protect your credentials and report suspected misuse.

9. Retention

Active Live Looks expire after the selected duration. Precise Live Look location evidence is designed to expire after 24 hours, and check-in proximity evidence is designed for limited retention. Account and social records remain while needed to provide the service or until deletion is completed. Security, moderation, de-identified crowd reports, backups, and legally required records may be retained longer. Exact production retention schedules and cleanup workers must be finalized before public launch.

10. Your controls

  • Choose profile, messaging, and nightlife-plan visibility.
  • Unfollow, block, report, remove your active Live Look, unsave venues, and sign out.
  • Deny or revoke camera, Photos, location, and notification permission in device settings.
  • Request an account data export or deletion from Profile → Account controls.

See Account Deletion & Privacy Choices for details.

11. Children and changes

GoHott is a nightlife-oriented service and is not directed to children. A final age-eligibility policy must be approved before public launch. Material policy changes will be posted here with a revised effective date.
