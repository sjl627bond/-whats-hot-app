Account, privacy, safety, and app help

Pre-launch contact

GoHott’s final monitored support email and response hours are not yet configured. During launch preparation, report technical issues through the project’s GitHub issue tracker. Do not include passwords, precise location, private messages, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Quick help

Account access

Check your email and password, network connection, and any confirmation email. Never share a password or session link.

Location

GoHott works without location. Change access in iOS Settings or browser site settings, then reopen the app.

Camera and Photos

Review GoHott’s permissions in iOS Settings. In the simulator, camera selection falls back to the photo library.

Safety and content

Use Report on a Live Look or profile, and Block to stop supported interactions. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger.

Account and privacy

Open Profile → Settings & Privacy to request a data export or begin authenticated account deletion. See Account Deletion & Privacy Choices for the exact flow and current release requirements.

Before requesting help

  • Note whether you are using Safari, installed PWA, simulator, or physical iPhone.
  • Include the screen and a short description of what happened.
  • Do not publish authentication links, personal identifiers, precise coordinates, or private content.
