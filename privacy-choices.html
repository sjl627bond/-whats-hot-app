Deletion, export, permissions, and visibility

Deletion is not immediate in the current launch build

The in-app control records a protected request. A privileged backend worker must revoke sessions, remove the Auth account, delete associated records and media, and confirm completion. That worker and a published completion timeframe remain required before public launch.

Request account deletion in GoHott

  1. Sign in to the account you want deleted.
  2. Open Profile from the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll to Account controls.
  4. Tap Request account deletion and confirm.
  5. The Profile screen will show the request status when available.

Open Profile

What deletion is designed to remove

After identity verification and completion by the protected deletion process, GoHott is designed to remove the Supabase Auth account and account-linked profile, saved venues, follows, blocks, conversation participation, messages sent by that account, nightlife plans, Live Looks and associated private media, reactions, owned notifications, device-notification registrations, export requests, and other user-owned relationship records.

Check-in reports may remain in de-identified form because the database removes the account identifier when the Auth user is deleted. Moderation/audit entries, reports needed for safety, fraud-prevention records, legally required records, and limited backups may be retained or de-identified where reasonably necessary. Content already seen or captured by another user cannot be recalled from that user’s device.

Before deletion

Request an export from Profile → Account controls if you want a copy of your information. Export generation also requires a protected backend worker and secure delivery. Removing the app or signing out does not delete the account.

Other privacy choices

  • Profile: choose public, followers-only, or private visibility.
  • Messages: select who may contact you; block accounts to stop supported interactions.
  • Plans: choose visibility separately for each nightlife plan.
  • Live Look: remove your active post or report another post.
  • Saved venues: unsave venues at any time.
  • Location, camera, Photos, notifications: deny or revoke access in iOS Settings or browser site settings. Core discovery remains available without location.

Need help?

Visit Support. During pre-launch, do not place passwords, precise location, private messages, or other sensitive data in the public technical-support channel.
